Designed to include

A route with more
ways in.

Tell us what you need when you enquire. With advance notice, we coordinate the right guide, access points and pace so deaf, blind and low-vision travellers can enjoy the same story.

Deaf & hard of hearing

We can request LIS-supported guiding where available. Ostia Antica also offers LIS and International Sign videos with Italian and English subtitles.

Blind & low vision

Tactile panels, touchable materials and guided routes are available at Ostia Antica. The forest ride can be arranged in tandem with a sighted pilot.

Mobility & comfort

The archaeological park has facilitated routes and an accessible museum ramp. We confirm surfaces, rest stops and transport details before the weekend.

Advance notice matters: Castello Chigi and Ostia Antica arrangements depend on the route, staffing and the date. We confirm tactile or LIS support with the venue before taking payment.
Your two-day story

From first coffee
to final toast.

Times are a relaxed working plan; your guide adapts the pace to the group, weather and opening hours.

Day 01 / Nature + art

09:00

Meet at EUR Fermi

Metro B · meet your guide and begin the ride towards Castel Porziano.

09:40

Ohana Bar Bistrot

Cappuccino + cornetto at a community-minded local social hub.

10:15

Pineta di Castel Fusano

Slow walk, local flora and fauna, plus a stretch of ancient Via Severiana.

13:00

Chigi Castle picnic

Bring your packed lunch and settle into the green area beside the castle.

14:30

Inside Castel Fusano

17th-century fortress and Pietro da Cortona interiors · indicative entry €25.

16:00

Fishermen's Channel

Cross the Canale dei Pescatori and discover the Borghetto dei Pescatori.

16:30

Your afternoon choice

Animal Farm (from €8) or Be Wild Park (from €10), both family-friendly.

19:00

Roman dinner at Il Frantoio

Meat or fish menu, local wine and a table near the pine forest.

21:00

Check in

Ostia Antica Park Hotel & Spa · your base for the night.

Day 02 / Rediscovering history

10:00

Breakfast at the hotel

Take the bus to Romagnoli / Scavi Ostia Antica.

11:00

Archaeological Park of Ostia Antica

Forum, baths, horrea, Capitolium and museum · full ticket €18.

13:00

Arianna al Borghetto

Roman comfort food a few steps from the archaeological site.

15:00

Julius II Castle + Ostia Antica hamlet

The hamlet appeared as fictional Solitano, Marcello Muratori's home town, in Emily in Paris. Then continue to the Sacred Island, the Roman ships and the imperial ports.

19:00

Zenzero Biorestaurant

Organic, locally sourced cooking in the Park of the Pond Channel.

21:30

Return to EUR Fermi

Take the Metro Mare and Metro B back to the original meeting point with your guide.

Summer edition: from 21–30 August, swap the afternoon choice for the Sagra della Tellina at the Fishermen's District, with seafood tasting and the Stella Maris procession.
